FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my health data secure on MediTrack?

Absolutely. MediTrack uses Firebase with bank-grade encryption. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We follow strict Firestore security rules ensuring only authorized users can access their own data.

Can I access MediTrack on my phone?

Yes! MediTrack is fully responsive and works beautifully on all devices — smartphones, tablets, and desktops. Just open your browser and log in from anywhere.

How do doctors get access?

Doctors are registered by clinic administrators. Once your admin adds you to the system, you receive login credentials and can immediately start creating prescriptions and managing patients.

What file types can I upload for reports?

You can upload PDF, JPG, PNG, and JPEG files. Each file is stored securely in Firebase Storage with a maximum size of 10MB per file.

Will I get notifications for medicine reminders?

Yes! MediTrack uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to send push notifications. You'll receive reminders for medicines, upcoming appointments, new prescriptions, and follow-up dates — right on your device.

Is MediTrack free to use?

MediTrack offers a free tier for patients. Clinics and hospitals can contact us for enterprise pricing with unlimited doctors, advanced analytics, and dedicated support.
